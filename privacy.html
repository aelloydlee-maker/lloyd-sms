Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how Lloyd (the "Service"), a personal assistant operated by its owner, Kian Jessup, a sole proprietor, collects and uses information in connection with its SMS text messaging.

Who this Service messages

Lloyd is a private personal assistant. It sends text messages only to its owner and operator — the single individual who set up the Service. The owner opts in by texting JOIN to (650) 469-8121 and replying Y to confirm they are 21 or older and want messages, as described on the Lloyd Text Messages page. You must be 21 or older to opt in. If anyone else texts Lloyd's number, the Service sends at most one automatic reply saying that number was not enrolled; after that it gets only the standard automatic keyword replies (HELP, STOP, START). The Service does not message the general public.

Information we collect

We collect the owner's mobile phone number, the contents of text messages exchanged with the Service, and a record of when the owner opted in or out, so that the Service can send plans and event options, booking results, and replies to the owner's own requests, and act on the owner's instructions. If anyone else texts Lloyd's number, Lloyd's own records keep that number, but not what they wrote, so that it replies to them at most once.

How we use information

Information is used solely to operate the personal assistant for its owner — to send messages the owner has asked for and to respond to the owner's messages. We do not use it for advertising.

Messaging

Message frequency varies based on the owner's activity and requests. Message and data rates may apply.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes, and text messaging opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. To operate the Service, message contents are processed on the owner's behalf by its service providers: Twilio, which delivers the text messages; Anthropic, which processes the owner's requests; and Google, which provides the owner's email and calendar. When the owner asks Lloyd to make a reservation or complete a task on a website, the details it needs go only to the venue or site the owner chose, only to complete it.

Opt-out

You can stop messages at any time by replying STOP. Reply HELP for assistance.
