Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how Lloyd (the "Service"), a personal assistant operated by an individual sole proprietor, collects and uses information in connection with its SMS text messaging.

Who this Service messages

Lloyd is a private personal assistant. It sends text messages only to its owner and operator — the single individual who set up the Service and entered their own mobile number into its configuration. The Service does not message the general public.

Information we collect

We collect the owner's mobile phone number and the contents of text messages exchanged with the Service, so that the Service can send weekend plans, event options, reminders, and replies, and act on the owner's instructions.

How we use information

Information is used solely to operate the personal assistant for its owner — to send messages the owner has asked for and to respond to the owner's messages. We do not use it for advertising.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. SMS opt-in and phone numbers are not shared with any third parties.

Opt-out

You can stop messages at any time by replying STOP. Reply HELP for assistance.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to aelloydlee@gmail.com.
