Lloyd Text Messages

Lloyd is a private personal assistant operated by its owner, Kian Jessup, a sole proprietor. It sends text messages only to its owner: plans and event options, booking results, and replies to the owner's own requests. Messages may include full, unshortened links to third-party event, restaurant and reservation pages (such as Resy or OpenTable), venue phone numbers, and suggestions of bars and venues that serve alcohol.

Lloyd is not a public service. This page shows exactly how its owner opts in and the messages the owner receives.

How the owner signs up

  1. Text JOIN to (650) 469-8121 to sign up for text messages from Lloyd.
  2. Lloyd replies asking for confirmation. Reply Y to confirm that you are 21 or older and want text messages from Lloyd.
  3. Lloyd confirms you are enrolled. Messages begin only after this step.

You must be 21 or older to sign up. Any other number that texts JOIN, START or Y gets one reply saying it was not enrolled; after that it gets only the standard automatic keyword replies (HELP, STOP, START).

The messages exchanged when signing up

After you text JOIN:

Lloyd: Reply Y to confirm you are 21+ and want texts from Lloyd, your personal assistant: plans, bookings and replies. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out.

After you reply Y:

Lloyd: You're enrolled. Expect plans, bookings and replies to your requests. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out.

Program details

  • Message frequency varies based on your activity and requests.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply HELP for help, or email aelloydlee@gmail.com.
  • Reply STOP at any time to opt out. You'll receive one confirmation and no further messages. To resubscribe later, text START.
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
